Our story

Art that belongs in everyday life

Lord’s Art & Crafts is the studio of Kenyan artist and maker Tyrone John. From original paintings and cast moulds to hand poured candles, natural soaps and liquid handwash, every piece is made with care, local inspiration and a simple belief: if it can be made, it can be art.

Whether you are a collector in Nairobi, a hotel in Mombasa or a buyer overseas looking for authentic Kenyan craft, we will make something worth keeping and deliver it safely to your door.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are you open 24/7?

Yes. You can browse and send an order or enquiry any time of day or night, every day of the week.

Do you deliver countrywide in Kenya?

Yes, we deliver to every county in Kenya using trusted courier and parcel services.

Do you ship overseas?

Yes, we ship worldwide with careful packaging. Send us your country and we will quote shipping.

What payment methods do you accept?

M-Pesa (send money to 0792 381 698) and Visa or Mastercard through a secure payment link we send you after confirming your order.

Can I commission a custom painting or product?

Absolutely. We accept custom paintings, moulds, candles, soaps and gift sets. Share your idea on WhatsApp or through the contact form.

How do I place an order?

Tap Order on WhatsApp on any product, use the checkout form, or email us. We confirm availability, price and delivery, then you pay and we ship.
