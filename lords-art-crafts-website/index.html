About the Artist, Tyrone John

Tyrone John is a Kenyan artist and maker creating paintings, moulds, candles, soaps, handwash and handcrafted art. Every piece is made with care, local materials where possible, and a belief that art belongs in everyday life as much as on the gallery wall.

Whether you are a collector in Nairobi, a hotel in Mombasa or a buyer overseas looking for authentic African art, we will make something worth keeping.
